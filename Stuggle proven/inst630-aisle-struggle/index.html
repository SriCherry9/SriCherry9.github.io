              Struggle bus: what Chart.js can and cannot do here

              Library: Chart.js 4.6.1 (vendored). No workarounds are used in this version.

              • Wanted: a Venn, matrix or heat map of shared ingredients and allergens.
              • Not supported: Chart.js has none of those chart types.
              • Shown instead: the built-in pie chart with default options. It gives counts only (only A / in both / only B), and cannot show overlap as overlapping shapes or name the items.
              • Accessibility: a canvas has no keyboard focus and nothing for a screen reader. The lists carry the data.
